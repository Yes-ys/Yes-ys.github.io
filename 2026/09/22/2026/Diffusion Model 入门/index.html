Generation paradigm (Diffusion)

motivation

Unconditioned Generations : Assume that there is nothing as input. We have some training set (some imgs) and we want to train the model generate imgs like them. (l1~l3 is based on this thing)

Suppose: sample of obsevations from $p_{data}$

Obejective: Generate new images from a distribution of images $p_{data}$

对上面的前提和目标做一些补充

前提实际上是我们有一批数据集${x_1,x_2,…,x_N}$，这批数据集背后可能存在一个确定的分布$x_i \sim p_{data}(x)$，我们不知道这个分布的具体形式，我们有的只是有限的样本

生成模型的目标是学习上面那个我们不知道的分布$p_{data}(x)$，从而让我们可以从数据集中进行“采样”，即生成新的数据（图像）

Unconditioned Generation中的没有输入指的是没有条件输入，即没有$p(x|y)$中的y，但实际上是有输入的，可以看作一个随机种子$z$，从而完成$x_j = p(z)$的采样。（考虑最简单的采样一批符合高斯分布的样本，我们可以先用一些程序语言的随机数生成器，然后把它们输入到概率分布的函数中）

In img generation, model will statrt from noise

why from noise:

  1. easy to sample (from some easy distribution)
  2. introduce radomness (random start point will not make the end point always be the same)
  3. gaussian distribution has nice properties

forward and backwards (a landmark paper: DPM)

  1. Add noise (forward process)
  • $x_{image} = x_0$ , do add noise operation for $x_0$ and we get $x_1$, repeatedly, $x_2,x_3,…,x_{t-1},x_t,…, \epsilon \approx x_T$
  • add noise operation: $q(x_t|x_{t-1})$ this process is defined by us so we know it
  1. denoise (reverse process)
  • use an operation $p_{\theta}(x_{t-1}|x_t)$ to denoise, we want to learn a model for $p_{\theta}$

what is the $x_t$? to represent the image

  1. image is composed of pixel with height and width
  2. pic covers three colors, i.e. red, blue and green. so pixel = (0 to 255,0 to 255,0 to 255)，（R,G,B）
  3. $x_t = (x^{(1)},x^{(2)},…,x^{(n)})^T$，each $x^{(i)}$ is a pixel, the size of $x_t$ is the dimension n times 3

Some prerequisite:

1-D space: $x \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, $x$ is a scalar, $\mu$ is the expectation and $\sigma^2$ is the variance

n-D space (for vector): $x \sim N(\mu,\Sigma)$, where $x = (x^{(1)},x^{(2)},…,x^{(n)})^T$ and $\mu = (\mu^{(1)},\mu^{(2)},…,\mu^{(n)})$

$\Sigma$ is the Covariance like:

$
\begin{pmatrix}
\Sigma^{(1,1)}&\Sigma^{(1,2)}&\cdots&\Sigma^{(1,n)}\
\Sigma^{(2,1)}&\Sigma^{(2,2)}&\cdots&\Sigma^{(2,n)}\
\vdots&\cdots&\ddots&\vdots\
\Sigma^{(n,1)}&\Sigma^{(n,2)}&\cdots&\Sigma^{(n,n)}
\end{pmatrix}
$

补充一些关于n维向量所服从的正态分布的知识

如果$x \sim N(\mu,\Sigma)$，则概率密度函数：

$p(x) = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^{n/2}|\Sigma|^{1/2}} exp[-\frac{1}{2}(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)]$

$\mu = (\mu^{(1)},\mu^{(2)},…,\mu^{(n)})$作为均值向量有如下性质：

  1. $E[x^{(i)}] = \mu^{(i)}$，每个分量表示对应维度随机变量的期望
  2. 决定了分布的中心位置，对分布起到“平移”作用
  3. $\mu$ 是n维空间中概率密度的最高点

$\Sigma$是协方差矩阵，其中的分量 $\Sigma^{(ij)} = Cov(x^{(i)},x^{(j)}) = E[(x^{(i)} - \mu^{(i)})(x^{(j)} - \mu^{(j)})]$

有如下性质：

  1. $\Sigma$是对称矩阵，对角线处就是对应随机变量的方差
  2. 非对角线元素描述了对应两个元素的线性相关性（正相关、负相关，以及线性相关性的强度）
  3. 整体决定了分布的情况

上面第3点性质在二维的情况下会比较好理解

考虑二维随机变量$(x^{(1)},x^{(2)})^T$，服从正态分布，协方差矩阵$\Sigma$如下，其中$\rho$是相关系数，也对应了一种协方差的计算方法

$
\begin{pmatrix}
\sigma_1^2&\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2\
\rho\sigma_1\sigma_2&\sigma_2^2
\end{pmatrix}
$

概率密度是一个三维空间中的曲面（随机变量对应两个维度），考虑等高线（相同概率密度值在曲面上截出来的曲线，在二维平面上的投影）

  1. 若$\rho > 0$则两个随机变量正相关，等高线是一个椭圆，沿着左下到右上（一个随机变量取值越大，另一个通常也越大）
  2. 反之，则椭圆沿着左上到右下
  3. 当$|\rho|$越接近1时，两个随机变量的线性相关性越强，对应的等高线椭圆越扁

N维正态分布的一些其他性质

  1. 线性变换之后仍然正态，$Ax+b \sim N(A\mu+b,A\Sigma A^T)$
  2. 边缘分布，主要针对分量，$x^{(i)} \sim N(\mu^{(i)},\Sigma^{(i,i)})$
  3. 条件分布，$x^{(i)}|x^{(j)} \sim N(\mu^{(i)} + \Sigma^{(ij)}(\Sigma^{(jj)})^{-1}(x^{j}-\mu^{j}),\Sigma^{(ii)}-\Sigma^{(ij)}(\Sigma^{(22)})^{-1}\Sigma^{(ji)})$

isotropic Gaussians is mainly used by us in diffusion model:

$x \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2 I)$

$\Sigma = \sigma^2 I$, let’s expand it:

$
\begin{pmatrix}
\sigma^{2}&0&\cdots&0\
0&\sigma^{2}&\cdots&0\
\vdots&\cdots&\ddots&\vdots\
0&0&\cdots&\sigma^{2}
\end{pmatrix}
$

  1. $\sigma^2$ is the varince of all the dimension, that is to say they are the same
  2. the probability density function is $p = \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma^2)^{n/2}}exp(-\frac{||x-\mu||^2}{2\sigma^2})$

noise representation is like:

$\epsilon = (\epsilon^{(1)},\epsilon^{(2)},…,\epsilon^{(n)})\sim N(0,I)$

  1. $0$ there is a vector, like the $\mu$ above

end of prerequisite

Let’s explore the details of adding noise:

q is choosen by us so it’s know

roughly describe:

$img_{noiser} = something \times img_{noise} + something\ else\times noise$

  1. easy to see: $something,something\ else,noise$ is all defined by us
  2. what we talk about in 1. is the $q(x_t|x_{t-1})$, it will be stochastic
  3. there is a recurring term noise schedule in papers which refers to the cofficients here ($something,something\ else$)

the detail of $q$, from $x_{t-1}$ to $x_t$ we use:

$q(x_t|x_{t-1}) = N(\sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1},\beta_t I)$

记录一个问题，之前那个带something的式子，说它用来生成noiser的图片是好理解的，我们也可以说它生成的图片的概率服从上述q的分布，但是当我们说使用上述概率分布来生成noiser的图片的时候实际上指的是什么？或者说知道这个概率分布之后怎么生成noiser的图片

the meaning of $q(x_t|x_{t-1})$:

  1. the center of noiser img is the less noise img with some coefficient
  2. when add some noise which is $something$ represented by the covariance matrix $\beta_t I$

behind the distribution, the actual formula of the generation from $x_{t-1}$ to $x_t$ is:

$x_t = \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1} + \sqrt{\beta_t}\epsilon,\epsilon \sim N(0,I)$

实际上这就是上面问题的答案，或者说我们先确定了这样一个生成式子，才有了$q$那样的分布

why we use a fomula above:

  1. this formula has a good quality of VP (variance preserve), that is to say the variance wouldn’t increase infinitely
  2. the opposites of VP is VE (varince exploding)

note:

  1. this cofficient $\beta_t$ isn’t typically uniform, they are varying like $0 \le \beta_1 < \beta_2 < … < \beta_T \le 1$
  2. the setting of $\beta_t$ in 1. is correspond to DDPM and it’s gradually increased, the reason of why we do that is when the img is near clean, we hope it change little and when it’s noisest, we hope the learning is the general shape of image (the varince of that distribution present the noise we wanna to add, like we have said before)

there is a way to omit the iterative steps, changing formula to:

$q(x_t|x_0) = N(\sqrt{\bar{a}_t} x_0,(1 - \bar{a}_t)I)$, with $\bar{\alpha}t = \prod^t{i=1}\alpha_i,\alpha_t = 1-\beta_t$

  1. the meaning of this formula is that we don’t need to noise our img in a sequential way, just make a step of t
  2. the key is to use the property of Gaussian distribution:
  • define $\alpha_t = 1-\beta_t$
  • expand $x_{t-1}$: $x_t = \sqrt{\alpha_t \alpha_{t-1}}x_{t-2} + \sqrt{\alpha_t(1-\alpha_{t-1})}\epsilon_{t-1} + \sqrt{1-\alpha_t}\epsilon_t$
  • use the property of Gaussian distribution: $x_t$ = $\sqrt{\alpha_t\alpha_{t-1}}x_{t-2} + \sqrt{1-\alpha_t\alpha_{t-1}}\epsilon$, where $\epsilon \sim N(0,I)$
  1. based on 2. we can cascade these things up to 0

补充，上面2.中的推导，使用高斯分布的性质，使用的是：两个独立零均值高斯噪声的线性组合，仍然是零均值高斯噪声，其方差等于系数平方和

  • 更宽泛的说，均服从高斯分布的两个独立随机变量$X \sim N(\mu_x,\Sigma_x),Y\sim N(\mu_y,\Sigma_y)$，有$aX+bY \sim N(a\mu_x+b\mu_y,a^2\Sigma_x+b^2\Sigma_y)$，利用这一点上面2.中最后一步推导是显然的

以及$x_t = …$中关于$\epsilon_{t},\epsilon_{t-1}$的部分本身就是描述的一个采样噪声的过程，当我们发现一个新的噪声分布的采样可以等价于以前两次采样，自然可以如此替代。

补充一些关于联合概率密度、边缘概率密度、条件概率密度的知识：

  1. 对于连续随机变量，当我们谈论$p$的时候，实际上是谈论的概率密度
  2. 在高维情况下的$p(x)$，实际上就是联合概率密度$p(x_1,x_2,…)$，后者各个随机变量是标量
  3. 联合概率密度对$Vol_d$（d维体积，d由随机变量的数量，或者说随机变量的维度确定）的积分总为1，即分别对各个随机变量在合法区间上的积分总为1；
  • 而一般的概率定义是$P(X\in A) = \int_A f_x (x)dx$，即随机变量在高维空间中给定区域的概率
  • 概率密度函数的定义是$f_x(x) = \lim_{\epsilon -> 0^+} \frac{P(X\in B_\epsilon (X))}{Vol_d(B_\epsilon (X))}$
  1. 边缘概率密度定义为对联合概率密度中一部分随机变量在合法区间上进行区分，得到的是剩余随机变量的边缘概率密度函数
  • $\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)dy = f_X(x)$
  1. 条件概率密度函数定义为概率密度函数除以作为条件的随机变量对应的边缘概率密度函数
  • $ \frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_X (x)} = f_{X|Y}(y|x)$
  1. 一个问题：如何理解二维情况下，联合概率分别对两个变量合法区间的积分值为1，而在确定一个变量的取值对应的条件概率在另一个变量合法区间的积分值也为1
  • 关键在于：固定一个变量后，条件概率密度需要重新归一化，它不等于直接从联合密度中取出的一条截面。
  • $ \int f_{X|Y}(y|x) dy = \frac{\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)dy}{f_X (x)} = \frac{f_X(x)}{f_X(x)} = 1$

a notation that seen very often in paper:

$p(x_1,x_2,…,x_T) = p(x_{1:T})$

based on this notation we can write the joint probabilty like:

$p(x_{1:T}) = p(x_1)\prod^T_{t = 2} p(x_t| x_{1:t-1})$, and this is the probabilty chain form

also marginalization: $p(x_1) = \int p(x_{1:T}) dx_{2:T}$

how to represent the $p_\theta (x_0)$, given our goals is to maxmize the $\log p_\theta (x_0)$

  1. based on marginalization: $p_\theta (x_0) = \int p_\theta(x_0,x_{1:T}) dx_{1:T}$

however it can’t be computed and we will use some tricks. understand it on a high level strategy:

1. find something that is easy to compute, so we can maximize the lower bound of our obejective:

  • $E_{x_0 \sim q(x_0)}[\log p_\theta (x_0)] \ge E_{x_{0:T} \sim q(x_{0:T})}[\log \frac{p_\theta(x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}]$, this is the Evidence Lower BOund (ELBO)
  • derivation key is to make $q$ appear: $p_\theta (x_0) = \int p_\theta (x_0,x_{1:T}) dx_{1:T} = \int q(x_{1:T}|x_0)\frac{p_\theta (x_0,x_{1:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}dx_{1:T}=E_{x_{1:T}\sim q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}[\frac{p_\theta (x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}]$
  • use Jensen’s inequality: $\log(E[\frac{p_\theta (x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}]) \ge E[\log \frac{p_\theta (x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}]$
  • the meaning of this trick: one way we progressed is instead of summing through all possible trajectories, we actually sampling from trajectories that were drawing from forward process, which we know.
  • 上面这句话是teacher对这个trick的meaning的解释，有点不太理解，进一步问了一下AI： 原始积分$\int p_\theta (x_0,x_{1:T})dx_{1:T}$ 需要把所有可能的轨迹都考虑进去（一条轨迹就是一组具体的中间图像$x_{1:T} = (x_1,x_2,….,x_T)$，即使固定了$x_0$，由于每步噪声不同，也会有无数条可能的轨迹，上面的积分把这些轨迹的联合概率密度都全部累积了起来），由于每个$x_t$都是高维图像，所以这是一个很高维度的积分，无法直接求解
  • 这个trick引入$q$，让积分变得能够通过采样估计期望：由于前向加噪过程$q$是我们定义的，对给定的$x_0$可以依次采样$x_1 \sim q(x_1|x_0),…,x_T\sim q(x_T|x_{T-1})$，于是得到了一条轨迹$x_{1:T} \sim q(x_{1:T}|x_0)$，由于我们有$p_\theta (x_0)=E_{x_{1:T}\sim q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}[\frac{p_\theta (x_{0:T})}{q(x_{1:T}|x_0)}]$，其中$x_{1:T}$可以通过上一步描述的采样得到，当得到一系列$x_{1:T}^{(k)}$之后，对于这个“期望”完全可以通过求平均进行估计
  • 即: $p_\theta (x_0) \approx \frac{1}{K}\sum^K_{k=1}\frac{p_\theta(x_0,x^{(k)}{1:T})}{q(x^{(k)}{1:T}|x_0)}$
  • 为什么使用ELBO？ 根据上面的估值方法，可以先计算期望再求对数，但是这样的估计一般有偏差，且密度比值波动大估计不稳定；而通过Jensen得到的ELBO在采样估计的视角下，就是一个对数比值平均：$\hat{ELBO(x_0)} = \frac{1}{K} \sum^K_{k=1}[\log p_\theta (x_0,x^{(k)}{1:T}) - \log q(x^{(k)}{1:T}|x_0)]$，这是比较稳定的
  • the intution: we will not sum through all trajectories, just summing through the trajectories sampled from the forward process

补充，关于上面的derivation中推导出期望那一步，结合期望的原始定义理解：

  1. 离散型，最初的定义，取值乘概率再求和，$E[X] = \sum_i x_i P(X = x_i)$
  2. 离散型，随机变量函数的期望，$E[X] = \sum_i f(x_i) P(X = x_i)$
  3. 连续型，随机变量函数，依赖概率密度$q(x)$
  • 考虑一个小区间，概率近似 $P(x \le X < x+\Delta x) \approx q(x)\Delta x$
  • 小区间构造加权和，$E[f(X)] \approx \sum_i f(x_i)q(x_i)\Delta x$
  • 对区间长度取极限，即得到积分，$E[f(X)] = \int f(x)q(x) dx$
  1. proving that term could be computed
  • refresher: KL divergence, $KL(P||Q) = \sum^n_{i = 1} p_i \log (\frac{p_i}{q_i})$, which is used to quantify how far apart the two distributions are.
  • KL divergence in continuous world: $KL(P||Q) = \int \log (\frac{p(x)}{q(x)})p(x) dx = E_{x\sim p}[\log (\frac{p(x)}{q(x)})]$
  • key: we can expand terms of lower bound: $ELBO = -\sum^T_{t = 2}KL(q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)||p_\theta (x_{t-1}|x_t)) + some other terms$

foucsed on the key:

the meaning of $q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)$ is that if we have the clean img and noise img, we want to know what will be the less noise img.

we wanna to know the distribution of the less noise img, and we can use Bayes’ Rule:

$q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0) = \frac{q(x_t|x_{t-1},x_0)q(x_{t-1}|x_0)}{q(x_t|x_0)}$

  1. $q(x_{t-1}|x_0),q(x_t|x_0)$ obvisiously could be computed based on the definition above
  2. $q(x_t|x_{t-1},x_0)$ could use Markov property, that is to say $q(x_t|x_{t-1},x_0) = q(x_t|x_{t-1})$, beacuse $q$ has the property of Markov
  3. $q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0) \propto q(x_t|x_{t-1},x_0)q(x_{t-1}|x_0)$ and $q(x_t|x_{t-1},x_0)q(x_{t-1}|x_0)$ is a normal distribution

the distribution of $q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0)$:

$q(x_{t-1}|x_t,x_0) = N(\tilde{\mu}_t,\tilde{\beta}_t)$

  1. $\tilde{\mu}_t = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}(x_t - \frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}}\epsilon_t)$, with $x_t$ is the noise img and $\epsilon_t$ is sampled noise, all of which are known
  2. $\tilde{\beta}t = \frac{1-\bar{\alpha}{t-1}}{1 - \bar{\alpha}_t} \beta_t$, notabley $\epsilon_t$ is the noise from $x_0$ to $x_t$ but not a sampled noise in step t, so $\epsilon_t = \frac{x_t - \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}}$

addtionally, $p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t)$ is tractable, the key is that we will assume it’s a Gaussian:

$p_\theta(x_{t-1}|x_t) = N(\mu_\theta(x_t,t),\Sigma_\theta(x_t,t))$

  1. Justification: if $q(x_t|x_{t-1})$ is a Gaussian then $q(x_{t-1}|x_t)$ is approx. Gaussian

let’s start deduce loss function

$L_{DDPM} = E_{t,x_0,t}[||\epsilon_\theta (\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon,t) - \epsilon||^2_2]$

  1. $t\sim \mathcal{U}{1,T}, x_0 \sim q_0(x_0), \epsilon \sim N(0,I)$
  2. $t$ represent a level of noise: $t$ is more towards $0$, which means we add less noise on a clean img and if it’s more towards $T$, it would mean more noise we have added
  3. $\epsilon$ is the actual noise we added from the clean img $x_0$ directly to the noisy img $x_t$
  4. $\sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon$ is equal to $x_t$
  5. $\epsilon_{\theta}$ is the added noise predicted by our model, which is based on a noise level $t$ and the noisy img $x_t$
  6. the meaning of this loss function is that we wanna to use the model $\epsilon_\theta$ to learn the actual noise $\epsilon$ we add on the clean img

how we go to the loss fuction from the ELBO?

look back to our strategy:

  1. derive lower bound for maximum (log-)likehood estimation
  • ELBO
  1. expand terms of lower bound
  • KL divergence
  1. show lower bound is tractable
  • Bayes rules & Gaussian property
  1. deduce final loss function

the fact is that we can simplify the scale divergence to end up with a weight coefficient times the expectation of the L2 regression, and the weight could be chosen humanly (1 is chosen by the athours of DDPM)

training and inference

training:

  1. Sample: clean img $x_0 \sim q_0(x_0)$, noise $\epsilon \sim N(0,1)$, time step $t \sim \mathcal{U}{1,T}$
  2. consitute noised img $x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon$
  3. use noised img $x_t$ and noise level $t$ to predict the noise $\epsilon$ via $\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)$
  4. compute the loss $L = ||\epsilon_\theta (x_t,t) - \epsilon||^2$ and backpropagate through $\epsilon_\theta$

inference:

  1. sample noise $x_T \sim N(0,I)$
  2. perform iterative update from $x_t$ to $x_{t-1}$
  • $x_{t-1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}(x_t - \frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}}\epsilon_\theta (x_t,t))+\sigma_t z,z \sim N(0,I)$
  1. obtain the final img $x_0$

补充，关于推理时的迭代公式：

  1. 为什么从$x_t$到$x_{t-1}$，不是直接减掉$\epsilon_\theta$，而是带有一些修正量
  • 因为网络预测的$\epsilon_\theta$实际上是构造$x_t$时的累积噪声，而非单步的噪声，因此要按照时间步的系数来调整这个量，因此有$\frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t} } \epsilon_\theta (x_t,t)$
  • 另外，我们在构造$x_t$的时候不仅累积了噪声，还对原始信号做了缩放，参考$x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha}_t}x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon$
  • 以及参考$x_t = \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1} + \sqrt{\beta_t}\epsilon,\epsilon \sim N(0,I)$
  • 因此有$x_{t-1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}(x_t - \frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}}\epsilon_\theta (x_t,t))+\sigma_t z$中的$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}$
  1. 关于这些修正量的具体推导，需要进行补充
  2. 为什么需要$\sigma_t z$
  • 理解这个首先要明确，模型本质上是要预测一个条件分布，并从中采样，而不是把条件均值作为唯一的答案。
  • 迭代更新的另一种等价形式是$x_{t-1} = \mu_\theta (x_t,t)+\sigma_t z,z \sim N(0,I)$，本质上是希望执行$x_{t-1} \sim p_\theta (x_{t-1}|x_t) = N(\mu_\theta(x_t,t),\sigma^2_t I)$ 的采样（给定$x_t$和$t$）
  • 也就是说从该采样可以推导出我们的迭代公式，这也是我们定义denoise过程的意义所在

Generation Paradigm (Score Match)

Motivation

Score Match is a new way to make generation paradigm different from diffusion

Goal: to sample a new img from the distribution of data set $p_{data}$

a common strategy: sample from an easy distribution and recover the img that we want to sample towards the end

details:

  1. we sample a $x$ which isn’t from $p_{data}$ but from a simple distribution, like Gaussian noise
  2. the key is we wanna to find a way that make us go to somewhere that is hard to sample like $p_{data}$ from somewhere that is easy to sample like $x$
  3. suppose we have the probabilty density function $p(x)$ that could give us the probabilty density value of $x$ we sampled
  4. easy to say that we can use gradient to get a $x$ that make $p(x)$ have a higher value

Some prerequisite:

Gradient of log of $p(x)$

we won’t directly use $\nabla p(x)$ because $\int p(x) dx = 1$ and it can be seen as $p(x) = frac{f(x)}{Z}$, where the $Z$ is intractable so $\nabla p(x) = \nabla \frac{f(x)}{Z}$ is still intractable

as well, $p(x)$ is easy to meet numerical issues because it will be very close to $0$ in low-density regions

A great idea is to use $\log p(x)$ instead of $p(x)$:

  1. $\nabla \log p(x) = \nabla (\log f(x) - \log Z) = \nabla \log f(x)$, therefore we could avoided computing $Z$
  2. $\log$ will make a fuction with numerical stability
  3. $\nabla \log p(x)$ represent the same direction of $\nabla p(x)$

补充：

要明白上面为什么反复提到一个$Z$，需要清楚一个常用的技巧：如果我们希望基于函数$f(x)$（满足非负），构造一个概率密度函数，常见的做法是令$p(x) = f(x)/Z$，其中$Z = \int f(u) du$，即所除以的归一化常数是$f$在整个空间上的积分。

而上面说直接使用$p(x)$的缺点就是$p(x)$也可以视作某个函数构造出的概率密度函数，在处理它的时候不可避免的要求某个复杂函数在整个高维空间上的积分，这通常是无法计算的，而对数化之后可以通过常数的梯度为0，来避免计算这个复杂积分，让其变得可以处理。

End of prerequisite

usually, we use the gradient $\log p(x)$ as the score function: $s(x) = \nabla_x \log p(x)$

now the question is should we use the score to go from a point easy to sample to highest density regions

the answer is we shouldn’t only go to the highest density regions

  • if we just follow the socre, we will get to the highest density regions
  • but there are also some points landed by the valid data from $p_{data}$ with lower density
  • we need to introduce some diversity

the way is called Langevin Sampling:

$x_t = x_{t-1}+\frac{\alpha}{2}\nabla_x \log p_{data}(x_{t-1})+\sqrt{\alpha}\epsilon_t$

  1. it will not only allow u go to the higher density regions but also allow u to make a bit exploration

到这里为止我有一个问题：上面一直在讨论，我们希望找到空间中$p(x)$较大的一些区域，我的问题是为什么$p(x)$较大的区域会更可能包含data set中的样本$x_i \sim p_{data}(x)$

实际上，上面讨论的$p(x)$可以直接理解为$p_{data}(x)$，即真实数据的目标分布，而两个体积相同的小领域中，密度较大的领域更加容易出现真实数据

而Langevin Sampling赋予了我们从某个位置出发，在假设知道真实的目标分布密度函数的梯度时，如何模仿真实的分布进行采样：

  1. $\frac{\alpha}{2} s(x_{t-1})$ 使得我们的位置向局部密度增大的方向移动
  2. $\sqrt{\alpha}\epsilon_k$ 是随机扩散，它和前者配合，会使得我们的采样在时间步$t$增长的角度看来，样本的长期分布符合目标分布$p_{data}$

总的来说问题被转化为了如何学习一个合理的$s_\theta (x)$来拟合$\nabla \log p_{data}(x)$

Score estimation

the ideal score estimation is to use this loss function:

$L_{SM} = E[||s_\theta (x) - \nabla_x \log p_{data}(x)||^2]$

however $\nabla_x \log p_{data}(x)$ is what we don’t know

there exist many ways to avoid the distribution $p_data$ and estimate the score

  1. Implicit Score Matching $L_{ISM} = E_x [\frac{1}{2}||s_\theta (x)||^2 + \nabla_x s_\theta (x)]$
  2. Sliced Score Matching $L_{SSM} = E_{x_0,t,\epsilon,v}[2v^T \nabla_x s_\theta (x)v+|v^Ts_\theta(x)|^2]$

now we will look into a common way used today, which is called denoising score match

let’s start from Gaussian Distribution:

for a 1-d Gaussian $x \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$:

Prbability distribution function:

$p(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi \sigma^2}}\exp (- \frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2 \sigma^2})$

we can easily get it’s actual socre:

$s(x) = -\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma^2}$

why this formula make sense?

  1. a typical Gaussian Distribution has the high density on the mean and lower density when we go away
  2. easy to see that any sampled x will be closer to $\mu$ by added the $s(x)$ so it makes sense

compute the score of Gaussian distribution is easy but it’s difficult for a not general distribution

now, we will consider computing the score of some samples form the Gaussian distribution but they are added some noise

Compute the socre of the Gaussian distribution with noise

$\tilde{x} = x + \sigma \epsilon,\epsilon \sim N(0,I)$

  1. $\epsilon$ is the noise from Gaussian distribution
  2. $\sigma$ is a scale factor to scale the noise
  3. $x$ is the sample from some typical distribution

$x$ is from Gaussian distribution,$\tilde{x}$ follows:

note: $\tilde{x}$ has a known distribution which mainly depends on known sample $x$ and $\sigma$ set by us, and it dosen’t depend on the distribution $p_{data}$

$q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x) = N(x,\sigma^2I)$

and it’s score function:

$\nabla_{\tilde{x}} \log q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x) = -\frac{\tilde{x} - x}{\sigma^2}$

  1. for the Gaussian distribution, the random varible $\tilde{x}$ by $x$ adding noise has a mean vlaue of $x$

now let’s conisder how to go back the start point about computing the score of $p_{data}$

the key is to take the $p_{data}$ distribution and noise it

define $q_\sigma (\tilde{x}) = \int q_\sigma (\tilde{x}|x)p_{data}(x) dx$

  1. $q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)$ is called perturbation kernel to discrible how you add the noise on each sample
  2. $p_{data}(x)$ means each sample in the origial distribution, that is to say we repeat the procedure of adding noise for all elements in $p_{data}$
  3. another denote is $q_\sigma (\tilde{x}) = q(\tilde{x},x)$
  4. in practice, we noly have a few data of the $p_{data}$, what we will do is to noise each samples i.e., $\tilde{x}i = x_i + \sigma \epsilon$, if $q{\sigma}(\tilde{x}|x) = N(x,\sigma^2 I)$
  5. $\sigma$ will control how much noise we add, small $\sigma$ means a little bit noise and bigger sigma means more noise to make sample spread out and takes much more space.

let’s try to use the definition to compte the true score

now we try to optimize the score matching objectives, which is like:

$L_{SM} = E_{\tilde{x}}[||s_\theta(\tilde{x})-\nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma (\tilde{x})||^2]$

some work has proved that it’s actually equal to optimize:

$L_{DSM} = E_{x,\tilde{x}}[||s_\theta(\tilde{x}) - \nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x)||^2]$

the derivation about it is use $||a - b||^2 = ||a||^2 - 2<a,b> + ||b||^2$, and the key is to prove the term about $<a,b>$ is the same

this is a good news! because the perturbation kernel is tractable, like $\nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma(\tilde{x}|x) = -\frac{\tilde{x} - x}{\sigma^2}$

actually the loss tractable above is called DSM(denoising score matching), denote $L_{DSM}$

Limitations with vanilla DSM

a idea is that we can use denoising score matching with a small sigma so the $q_{sigma}$ will very close to the original distribution $p_{data}$

however if we use a very small $\sigma$, we only have a poor estimation of the score in low-density regions

why does this happen?

we want to optimize $L_{SM} = E_{\tilde{x}}[||s_\theta(\tilde{x})-\nabla_{\tilde{x}}\log q_\sigma (\tilde{x})||^2]$

what that means is that we want to optimize on the loss that is around the distance between the predicted score and the score of the noise distribution $q$, which is equal to:

$L = \int ||\Delta_\theta (\tilde{x}) - \nabla \log q_\sigma (\tilde{x})||^2 q_\sigma (\tilde{x})d \tilde{x}$

  1. we weight our loss function with respect to how likely our points $\tilde{x}$ are to appear
  2. if $\tilde{x}$ is not likely to appear, then the factor it is off from the true value is not something that is going to be materially noticeable in the loss, which makes the model will not really be incentivized

说明：

  1. 原始的损失形式是期望，上面$L$的写法就是将期望按照积分的形式展开
  2. 这个损失的含义是，按照$\tilde{x}$的真实出现频率，对预测的误差求平均，在某一个小区域内，如果出现的频率大，也就是$q_\sigma (\tilde{x})$大，对应区域求得的$||\Delta_\theta (\tilde{x}) - \nabla \log q_\sigma (\tilde{x})||^2$对总体损失函数的值就比较大
  3. 进而，会引发的问题是，假设实际上目前模型预测效果很差，损失函数本应该较大，但是由于目前样本形成的$\tilde{x}$所出现的区域中，模型预测的表现效果比较好（假设这一部分只占了总体区域的百分之1），那么损失函数会较小，看起来模型已经优化好了，但是实际上在其余百分之99的区域，只要取了对应的$\tilde{x}$损失函数就会非常大，模型表现仍然很差
  4. 训练样本集中在真实图片附近；但生成时，我们往往从简单的高斯噪声开始。这样的初始点可能远离小噪声数据分布的主要区域。此时，我们希望 score 能提供可靠的方向，把样本逐渐带向数据区域。可是模型在这些起始位置缺少训练，预测的方向就可能不准确。

this is a trade-off: small $\sigma$ means the distribution $q_\sigma$ (whose score function we use) is closer to true distribution $q_{data}$ which we want, but the trained model have a poor ability to predict an accurate socre function in some general position (like the point we start from, when we want to make a img generation); big $\sigma$ means $q_\sigma$ is more different from $q_{data}$, but the score predicted by the model of $q_\sigma$ in some general position is more accurate

Score matching with varying noise via NCSN

to overcome the problem in last section, a great idea is:

we are not goint to only rely on one kind of noisy distribution, and we’re going to actually rely on all of the sigma from a lower value to a higher value

therefore we can release a effect that in the very beginning we know roughly where to go and the more we get closer to the high density region of the true distribution we know more and more about the details

the method is called NCSN = Noise Conditional Score Network:

  1. we estimate score $s_\theta (x,\sigma_i)$ at different noise level $\sigma_1 < … < \sigma_T$
  2. we use the loss: $L_{NCSN} = \sum^L_{i = 1} \lambda(i) E_{x\sim q_{\sigma_i}} [||s_\theta (x,\sigma_i) - \nabla_x \log q_{\sigma_i}(x)||^2_2]$

NCSN could predict an accurate score under different noise level and if we wanna to leverge these scores to sample step by step until get a good result $x_t$, we need to use ALD = Annealed Langevin Dynamics (another sampling method)

ALD:

  1. sample noise $x_T \sim N(0,\sigma^2_T I)$, $\sigma_T > … > \sigma_1$
  2. For each $\sigma_i$, perform iterative update for K steps:
  • $x \leftarrow x+\frac{\alpha_i}{2} s_\theta (x,\sigma_i) + \sqrt{\alpha_i}\epsilon$
  1. obtain final image $x_0$

Parallel between noise (DDPM) and score (NCSN)

a natural question is how dose the score relate to the noise prediction task

we know DDPM is about learn how to denoise under a permise that we have added the noise

  1. we add the noise by $x_t = \sqrt{\bar{\alpha_t}}x_0 + \sqrt{1- \bar{\alpha}_t}\epsilon$
  2. by the formula above, $q(x_t|x_0) = N(\sqrt{\bar{\alpha_t}}x_0,(1-\bar{\alpha}_t)I)$

the actul reality is that: the score of the distribution $q(x_t|x_0)$ is equal to the noise we added over some coefficient:

$\nabla \log q(x_t|x_0) = -\frac{\epsilon}{\sqrt{1-\bar{\alpha}_t}}$

  1. actually these two methods are two different ways of noising our sample (one is use weighted average between clean img and noise, and another one is just use sigma for clean img)
  2. this is why DDPM is seen as variance presevring (the variance of $x_t$ in DDPM is roughly equal to $1$), but NCSN is going to be mentioned as being variants exploding (because we’re going to add more and more noise (using $\sigma$), which is not bounded by $1$)

Continous formula

Up to now, noising img by us uses a discrete set of noise increments

So one idea is to acutally consider continous evolution instead of discrete one

(the reason why we wanna to do this is beacuase we have a filed of math which is differential equations that have a bunch of convenient toolkits to solve these kinds of equations)

补充：

  1. continous formula 连续时间形式，可以理解为把按照$t = 1,2,3,…,T$的离散时间步加噪，推广为“图像随着连续时间t不断受到微小的随机扰动”
  2. 它为 DDPM 和基于 score 的生成方法提供了统一描述，这个统一框架通常用随机微分方程SDE表示。
  3. 这样做的优点：
  • 统一不同方法。 DDPM 的加噪过程和 NCSN 使用的多噪声尺度，可以放进同一个 SDE 框架。
  • 更灵活地设计采样算法。 给定连续变化规律后，可以选择不同的数值求解器、时间网格和步长。
  • 更方便分析生成过程。 例如研究概率分布怎样随时间变化，以及它与确定性 ODE 的关系。

Wiener process

let’s try continous equivalent of adding increments of Gaussian noise

Wiener process is a type of stochasitc process which is a family of random varibles that are indexed by some index t

It needs to follow some special properties:

  1. $W_0 = 0$
  2. $W_t - W_s \sim N(0,(t-s)I)$
  3. independent increments

补充：

  1. Wiener process是维纳过程（也称标准布朗运动），可以记做${W_t}_{t \ge 0}$，有两种理解角度：
  • 固定一个时刻$t$，$W_t$是一个随机变量
  • 观察一次实验的全部时刻，得到一条随机轨迹$t -> W_t$
  • (可以想象：运行一次程序，生成一条随机变化的曲线；重新运行一次，又生成另一条曲线。维纳过程描述的是这些随机曲线所遵循的概率规律。)
  1. 关于维纳过程要遵循的三条性质：
  • 从零开始，表示最初没有积累任何随机扰动
  • 任意时间区间上是高斯分布
  • 第三条指的是，不相交时间区间上的增量相互独立，即$W_1 - W_0, W_2 - W_1$是相互独立的，但是注意$W_0,W_1,W_2$并非独立的（考虑$W_2 = W_1 + (W_2-W_1)$，并结合性质2就可以得出这个结论）
  • 实际上还有第四条性质，即轨迹连续，时间连续，轨迹不能出现跳跃

now let’s derive the continous formula of DDPM:

  1. starting from decrete formula:
  • $x_t = \sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta_t}\epsilon$
  • $x_t - x_{t-1} = (\sqrt{1-\beta_t} - 1)x_{t-1}+\sqrt{beta_t}\epsilon$
  1. define a quantity: $\beta = \beta(t)dt$
  • $\beta(t)$ is the rate of noise at time t
  • $dt$ is an increment of time
  1. to get a continous formula
  • $x_t - x_{t-1} = (\sqrt{1-\beta(t)dt}-1)x_{t-1}+\sqrt{\beta(t)dt}\epsilon$
  • tend $t$ to $0$ and consider a continous evolution, $x_t - x_{t-1}$ is going to be a differential evolution $dx_t$
  • when $dt$ tends to $0$, we have Taylor expansion formulas: $dx \sim (1 - \frac{1}{2}\beta(t)dt - 1)x + \sqrt{beta(t)}dW$ where the $dW = \sqrt{dt}\epsilon$ because $dW$ follows a normal distribution of variance $dt I$
  • finally we get: $dx = -\frac{1}{2}\beta xdt + \sqrt{\beta}dW$

this equation is a formula of Stochastic Differential Equation(SDE)

$dx = f(x,t)dt+g(t)dW$

  1. $f(x,t)$ is called drift and it’s deterministic
  2. $g(t)$ is called diffusion which is used to control the strength of randomness term (like $dW$)

just see how the SDE is used:

  1. suppose we have a start point $x$ and only have the drift term:
  • we will have a determistic evolution using $x <- x+dx$, it finally will be a determistic route
  1. if we consider the whole terms, a final step will include a determistic step and a stochastic step

therefore in DDPM’s continous formula:

  • $f(x,t) = -\frac{1}{2}\beta(t)x$
  • $g(t) = \sqrt{\beta(t)}$

NCSN’s continous formula:

  • $f(x,t) = 0$
  • $g(t) = \sqrt{\frac{d(\sigma^2(t))}{dt}}$

trianing and inference

now we know how to add noise with a continous formlua, but we care more about is how to denoise

traning obejective is alos predict the socre:

$L_{DSM} = E_{t,x_0,x_t}[\lambda_t||s_\theta (x_t,t)-\nabla_{x_t}\log p(x_t|x_0)||^2]$

training process:

  1. sample clean img $x_0 \sim p_{data}$, noise $\epsilon \sim N(0,I)$ and time step $t\sim \mathcal{U}(0,T)$
  2. noised img $x_t = \alpha_t x_0 +\sigma_t \epsilon$
  3. use $x_t$ and $t$ to predict $-\frac{\epsilon}{\sigma}$ via $s_\theta (x_t,t)$
  4. compute loss $L = \lambda_t ||s_\theta(x_t,t)+\frac{\epsilon}{\sigma_t}||^2$ and backpropagate through $s_\theta$

说明：

  • 这一部分是重复回顾了一下我们之前学过的$L_{DSM}$，训练的网络用于预测的是一个socre项
  • 结合后面的推理理解，因为推理的时候要用到这个score项

for inference, we need the reverse of the original continous formula above, which represents denoising process

$dx = [f(x,t)-g(t)^2\nabla_x \log (p(x))]dt + g(t)d\bra{W}$

  1. this is the advantages of continous formula: the forward process described as SDE can be reversed through a reverse SDE
  2. the socre term $\nabla_x \log (p(x))$ has appereared in the reverse SDE
  3. $d\bar{W}$ means that it’s different from $dW$

inference:

  1. sample noise $x_T \sim N(0,\sigma^2_TI)$
  2. use Euler-Maruyama to go through reverse SDE: $x_{t_{i-1}} = x_{t_i} + [f(x_{t_i},t_i) - g(t_i)^2 s_\theta(x_{t_i},t_i)]\Delta t + g(t_i)+\sqrt{\Delta t}\epsilon_i$
  3. Obtain final img $x_0$

说明：上面的i指的是可以在求解数值解的时候设置的迭代次数（Euler-Maruyama本身就是一种数值求解法）
